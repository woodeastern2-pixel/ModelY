## Provenance and limits

Document SHA-256 digests, original media names, source block positions, image dimensions and transcription confidence are included in assets/manuals/brity_documents.json. All images are bundled in lossless WebP archives and can be enlarged in Knowledge Materials. Pixel comparisons on white and black backgrounds matched all 911 source images.

Text and tables preserve the source wording. Machine-read screen text is a labelled supplement and may contain recognition errors; the original image remains available. Document-specific conditions and conflicting requirements are kept with their source rather than merged into a new policy.

The pack is installed once in a transaction at database open. Existing user records are not replaced. The installed marker prevents deleted manual records from being silently recreated.
